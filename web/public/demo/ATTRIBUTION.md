# Demo song attribution

`singer-songwriter/` was produced by running `reverser analyze` on the excerpt
`00_SS3-98-C_comp` (microphone recording) from **GuitarSet**:

> Q. Xi, R. Bittner, J. Pauwels, X. Ye, J. P. Bello. "GuitarSet: A Dataset for Guitar
> Transcription". ISMIR 2018. https://guitarset.weebly.com · https://zenodo.org/records/3371780

GuitarSet is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The audio files here are re-encoded (AAC) and passed through Demucs stem separation;
the tab, chords and MIDI are this project's automatic transcription, not the dataset's
ground truth.
